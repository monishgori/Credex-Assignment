# Go To Market (GTM)

## Target User
**Exact Target**: VP of Engineering or Director of Engineering at Series A or Series B startups (headcount 20-100).
**Why**: At this stage, teams are large enough that individual subscriptions add up to thousands of dollars, but they rarely have a dedicated procurement team policing SaaS spend.

## Search Intent
They aren't Googling "AI tool audit." They are Googling:
- "How to manage GitHub Copilot licenses"
- "ChatGPT Enterprise pricing vs Team"
- "Cursor vs Copilot for team"

## Online Hangouts
- Private Slack communities: Rands Leadership Slack (Engineering Managers), CTO Craft.
- Subreddits: r/ExperiencedDevs, r/SaaS.

## First 100 Users (Zero Budget)
I will do direct cold outbound to VPEs on LinkedIn with a hyper-personalized message. 
"Hey [Name], noticed [Company] has ~40 engineers. If you're paying retail for Copilot or ChatGPT, you're likely overspending by 20%. I built a free calculator to check if your current license allocation is optimal. Takes 30 seconds."

## The Unfair Channel
**Credex's existing network**: Since Credex already has access to discounted AI infrastructure credits, we can leverage our existing partner network (VC firms) to distribute this tool to their portfolio companies as a "free perk/health check."

## Week-1 Traction Metrics
If this works, Week 1 looks like:
- 500 unique visitors
- 150 completed audits (30% conversion rate on the form)
- 30 emails captured for audits showing >$500/mo savings
- 5 consultations booked
