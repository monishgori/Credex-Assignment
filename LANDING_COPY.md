## FAQ
**1. How does this work?**
You input your current tools, team size, and spend. Our engine cross-references this against live pricing data to find cheaper plans, unused allocations, or discounted credits.

**2. Do I need to connect my billing account?**
No. The audit is 100% manual input and requires no integration with your current providers.

**3. Is this really free?**
Yes. The audit is free. If we find massive savings, we hope you'll use Credex to provision the cheaper infrastructure, but there is no obligation.

**4. What tools do you track?**
We currently track pricing for Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Windsurf.

**5. How accurate is the pricing data?**
Our pricing database is updated weekly directly from the vendors' official pricing pages.
