# Unit Economics

## Value of a Converted Lead
If a lead books a consultation and purchases infrastructure credits through Credex, the average deal size for a 50-person engineering team is roughly $1,000/month in credits. Assuming a 20% margin for Credex, that's $200/month or **$2,400 LTV** (assuming a 12-month retention).

## CAC by Channel
- **Cold Outbound (LinkedIn)**: Tooling costs (Apollo/PhantomBuster) ~$100/mo. Generates 5 leads. CAC = **$20**.
- **VC Partner Channel**: Free distribution. CAC = **$0**.

## Conversion Funnel required for Profitability
- 1,000 Audits Completed
- 10% Email Capture Rate = 100 Leads
- 10% Consultation Booking Rate = 10 Consultations
- 20% Close Rate = 2 Deals
- Profit = 2 * $2,400 = $4,800.
Since the tool costs almost nothing to run (Serverless hosting), this funnel is highly profitable.

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 Months
To reach $1M ARR, we need $83,333 in MRR. 
At $200 MRR profit per customer, we need **416 active customers**.
Working backwards through the funnel:
- 416 customers require 2,080 consultations (20% close)
- 2,080 consultations require 20,800 captured leads (10% booking)
- 20,800 leads require 208,000 completed audits (10% capture)
To achieve this in 18 months, the tool needs to process ~385 audits per day. This requires expanding distribution beyond outbound into SEO and programmatic ad spend.
